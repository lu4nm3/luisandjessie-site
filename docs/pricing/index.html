Pricing

We believe in transparency and won’t make you jump through hoops to get to our pricing.

Below you will find all the details for everything we offer. If you’re looking for slightly different options, please contact us and we can work with you to provide a more customized experience.

Even if your budget doesn’t fully align with our pricing, it never hurts to get in touch and ask! We are usually able to work something out!

What’s included

* Unlimited high resolution images

Unlike other photographers, we do not impose any artificial limits on the number of images we deliver. You’ll get pretty much every shot we take.

* Image retouching

Every single photo we deliver will be individually retouched and edited with our unique vivid filmic style, which will preserve all of the colors and tones as you remember them but with an added “oomph”.

* Online image gallery

Your images will be delivered in an online gallery that you can share with friends and family. From there, you’ll also have access to order prints, photo albums, and a lot more.

* Unrestricted printing rights

All images we deliver in your online gallery will be yours to print, share, and post online.

* Timeline planning and assistance

We will work with you and your coordinator to make sure your wedding goes off without a hitch while making sure we capture all of those important moments.

* Quick turnaround

We will deliver a few select “sneak peak” images within 3-days while we work on the rest of your gallery which you can expect 6-8 weeks afterwards.

RAW Images

$1,000

RAW files are the uncorrected data files created by modern digital cameras. If you were a painter, RAW files would be the equivalent of your sketches and unfinished canvases before your final artistic strokes are applied. It’s important to note that these image files will look very different from those in your gallery. This is something that very few clients ask for, but it’s something we’re willing to provide as long as you know what you’re getting.

  • All RAW files of the images in your gallery
